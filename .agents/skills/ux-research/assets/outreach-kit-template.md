# Recruitment kit: [study name]

Deliver copy-paste-ready messages in code blocks, and the checklist sections as plain markdown.

## 1. Email invitation

```text
Subject: [X] minutes to shape [product/experience]?

Hi [First name],

We're improving [product/experience] and we want to base the changes on how people actually use it.

Would you take part in a [duration] session on [format: video call / in person]? You'd walk us through how you [do the relevant task] today and react to some early ideas.

- Duration: [X] minutes
- Format: [video call / in person]
- Prep needed: none
- There are no right answers — we're testing the tool, not you
- [Recording: the session is recorded for note-taking only, and results are reported anonymously]
- [Incentive, if external]

Pick a slot that works for you: [booking link]

Thanks a lot,
[Name] — [Role]
```

## 2. Chat / Slack invitation

```text
Hi [First name] 👋 We're improving [product/experience] and we'd love your perspective.

[X] minutes, [video call], no prep. You show us how you [task] today and react to a few ideas. No right answers — we're testing the tool, not you. Results are anonymous.

Book a slot here: [booking link]
```

## 3. Confirmation message

```text
Thanks for booking — you're confirmed for [date] at [time] ([duration]).

Link: [meeting link]

What to expect: a short intro, then you showing us how you [task] today, then a few ideas to react to. Nothing to prepare.

[If screen sharing: You'll be asked to share your screen, so please close anything you'd rather not show and mute notifications.]

[Recording and consent note.]

If something comes up, just reply here and we'll reschedule.
```

## 4. Reminder (24 hours before)

```text
Quick reminder — our [duration] session is tomorrow at [time]. Link: [meeting link]
Nothing to prepare. See you then!
```

## 5. Thank-you message

```text
Thanks for your time today — genuinely useful. Everything you shared is anonymized before it goes any further.

We'll share what we learned with you around [date]. [Incentive delivery note, if applicable.]
```

## 6. Calendar setup checklist

- Use an appointment-schedule / booking page so participants self-serve real availability.
- **Event title:** [Clear and specific, e.g. "Feedback session: [Product]"]
- **Duration:** [X] minutes, with a buffer between sessions
- **Location:** [meeting link or room]
- **Guests:** participant, moderator, notetaker (observers optional and silent)
- **Description to paste into the invite:**

> **What we'll do:** a short intro, then you showing us how you [task] today, then reactions to a few early ideas.
>
> **Nothing to prepare.** There are no right answers — we're testing the tool, not your skills.
>
> [Recording and confidentiality note.]

- Enable automatic reminders 24 hours before.

## 7. Ops checklist

- Over-recruit by ~20% or keep a backup list for no-shows.
- Check RSVPs 24 hours before; send one friendly nudge to anyone pending.
- Internal studies: have the PM align with the participant's manager so the time is protected.
- Internal studies: no monetary incentive — commit to sharing results back instead.
- Test the prototype, accounts, and recording setup before the first session.
- Keep a recruitment tracker: participant ID, segment, status, session date, consent received.
