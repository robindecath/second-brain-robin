## Welcome and consent

> 🗣️ **Read aloud:** "Thanks for making the time. We're working on [topic] and we want to understand how it works for you in practice. This will take about [X] minutes.
>
> Two things before we start. First, we're testing the tool, not you — if anything is confusing, that's a problem with our design, not with you, and that's exactly what we need to hear. Second, there are no right answers, you can skip any question, and you can stop at any time.
>
> [We'd like to record the session for note-taking. Only the research team sees the recording, and anything we share is anonymized.] Is that okay with you?"

☐ Verbal consent obtained before recording starts

## Warm-up

> 🗣️ **Read aloud:** "To start, tell me a bit about your role and what a typical [day/week] looks like for you."

> 🗣️ **Read aloud:** "When was the last time you [relevant task]? Walk me through what happened."

**Observe:** vocabulary they use, frequency, context, who else is involved.
